## Research

**World Models for Autonomous Navigation**  
*University of Nottingham Ningbo China, with Dr. Huan Jin's group*

* Collected about 80 GB of campus video and sensor data and built a spatiotemporally aligned dataset of over 20,000 frames, using relative coordinates and yaw angles to describe the robot's motion.
* Reproduced a CDiT-based navigation world model and trained the NoMaD diffusion policy, balancing action and distance losses with dynamic loss scaling.

**Deep Reinforcement Learning for Instant-Retail Supply-Chain Disruption**  
*Supervised by Dr. Ruijing Wu* · [[Code]](https://github.com/maoyidingnb-png/rl_supply_chain)

* Modelled a network of one central warehouse, ten forward warehouses and five product categories as a Markov decision process, with actions combining inventory buffering, expedited transportation and scarce-stock allocation.
* Benchmarked DQN, DDQN, Dueling DQN, D3QN and PPO, using Common Random Numbers and independent training seeds for fair comparison.
